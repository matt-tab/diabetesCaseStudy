****Analyzing Associated Factors That Contribute to Diabetes****

**Goal:** To Determine what factors contribute most to a successful diagnosis of diabetes

**Description:**  This project analyzes the relationship between various health indicators and the likelihood of a diabetes diagnosis among Pima Indian patients in Phoenix, Arizona. The dataset includes factors such as Glucose, Pregnancies, Blood Pressure, Skin Thickness, Insulin, BMI, and Age. This project involved loading the data, conducting preprocessing and data cleaning, performing exploratory analysis (EDA), analyzing the correlation between budget and gross earnings, and implementing machine learning techniques (K-NN, Logistic Regression Modeling) and statistical analysis.

**Skills:** R, Statistical Analysis, Machine Learning, Prediction Modeling

**Results:** Using R, the analysis revealed that Age, BMI, and Glucose Concentration are the most powerful indicators in early diabetes detection. However, many other variables had strong correlations with each other (e.x Pregnancies and Age) that warrant further analysis.
